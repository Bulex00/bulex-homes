Our Story

Founded in 2016 by Adegbite Abdullahi, Bulex Homes began with a simple vision: to transform the way Nigerians experience luxury real estate. What started as a small boutique agency in Lekki has grown into one of Lagos most trusted names in premium property sales and rentals.

Over the last ten years, we have helped over a thousand families and investors find their perfect properties across Lagos most coveted neighborhoods. Our deep understanding of the local market, combined with international standards of service, sets us apart in the competitive Lagos real estate landscape.

Today, Bulex Homes represents the finest collection of luxury properties in Lagos, from waterfront estates in Banana Island to contemporary penthouses in Victoria Island. Our team of 50+ expert agents brings decades of combined experience and an unwavering commitment to client satisfaction.
