Privacy Policy

At Bulex Homes, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information including your name, email address, phone number, and viewing preferences when you contact us, book a viewing, or subscribe to our newsletter. We also use localStorage to save your preferences such as dark mode, favorites, and recent searches entirely on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule property viewings
  • To send property updates and market insights (with your consent)
  • To improve our website experience and property recommendations
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage

Personal data you submit through forms is stored securely and never sold to third parties. Preferences saved via localStorage remain on your browser and are not transmitted to our servers.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to ensure the website functions correctly. With your consent, we may use non-essential cookies to analyze site usage and improve our services.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party platforms such as WhatsApp, Facebook, and LinkedIn. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@bulexhomes.ng.
